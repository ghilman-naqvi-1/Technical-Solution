Evaporative Central Cooling System

Eco-Friendly Evaporative Cooling Solutions

Evaporative cooling systems offer an energy-efficient and environmentally friendly alternative to traditional air conditioning. Our team specializes in the installation and maintenance of central evaporative cooling systems that use natural evaporation to cool your space effectively.

What We Offer

System Installation

Complete installation of ducted evaporative cooling systems for whole-home comfort.

Pad Replacement

Regular cooling pad replacement to maintain optimal cooling efficiency.

Seasonal Service

Pre-season startup and winterization services for year-round care.

Repairs

Expert diagnosis and repair of all evaporative cooler components.

Benefits of Evaporative Cooling

  • Up to 75% lower energy costs compared to traditional AC
  • Environmentally friendly with no harmful refrigerants
  • Fresh air circulation instead of recirculated air
  • Natural humidity addition in dry climates
  • Lower installation and maintenance costs
  • Ideal for hot, dry climate regions
